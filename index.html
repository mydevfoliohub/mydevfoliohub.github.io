BEFORE YOU BUILD

A few things
you might be wondering.

Less setup. More room for your work.

Do I need to know how to code?＋

No. Create your account, fill in your profile and add your work from the dashboard. You do not need to edit HTML or set up hosting.

Is this only for developers?＋

It is for people across technology: students, developers, security learners, data enthusiasts and more. Use the sections that fit your journey.

Can I update my portfolio later?＋

Yes. Return to your dashboard to edit your profile, add projects, record new lessons or change your portfolio theme.

How do I share my portfolio?＋

Your username gives you a personal public link. Copy it from your dashboard and add it to your resume, social profiles or messages.
